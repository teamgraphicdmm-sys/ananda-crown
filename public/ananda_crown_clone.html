ABOUT US

Ananda Crown is built on a simple belief, that ultra-luxury living should feel considered, not crowded.
Four standalone towers, connected only at the base and the crown, rise above Sector 78 with a design language that prioritises light, space, and privacy over density. This is the group's first project outside Bathinda, and its most ambitious yet, led by an IIT Delhi and IIM Ahmedabad founding team, and a Delhi-based design and consultant roster.

Life at
Ananda Crown

Ananda Crown was designed around a single idea, that a home should give you space, not just shelter you. The ground level here has no vehicles at all, every car is parked below, so the space between the towers stays open, landscaped, and genuinely usable. Positioned in Sector 78, residents stay close to Chandigarh International Airport, IT City/Aerocity, the I.S. Bindra PCA Cricket Stadium, ISB Mohali and IISER, VR Punjab Mall, Elante Mall, and Fortis and other multi-speciality hospitals, near everything that matters, without being in the middle of it.
