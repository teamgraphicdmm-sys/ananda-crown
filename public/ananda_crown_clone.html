ABOUT US

BUILT IN BATHINDA. Raised in Mohali.
For years, families in Bathinda have lived in homes Ananda Group promised and delivered. Crown is the next chapter: the same commitment, now raised to 29 storeys in Mohali. Since 1995, the group has developed land, homes and commercial spaces across Punjab: investing early, building infrastructure first and delivering on the promised date. Ananda Crown in Sector 78 is its first project beyond Bathinda.
